Frequently asked questions

A clear answer beneath the moonlight.

These answers describe the fictional website prototype, not a live hotel's policies or availability.

Is Lunaire a real hotel?

No. Lunaire is a fictional website and hospitality concept. Its rooms, prices, facilities, location details, and services are sample content.

Does the booking form create a reservation?

No. It creates a local demonstration request only. It does not check availability, contact a hotel, send email, or process payment.

Can I enter my card details?

No. Do not enter card numbers, banking information, passwords, or other sensitive information anywhere in this prototype.

Can I change my dates?

Yes. In the demonstration, use the Edit dates control or restart the booking flow. A real implementation would depend on availability and rate conditions.

Are the rates and packages real?

No. All rates, packages, taxes, fees, capacities, and inclusions are conceptual examples for design and development.

Can I request accessibility support?

The booking prototype includes an accessibility requirements field. In a real implementation, requests would be reviewed and confirmed rather than guaranteed.

How can I contact Lunaire?

Use the sample contact form for demonstration purposes. No real contact address or property team is represented by this website.
