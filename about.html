About Lunaire · Founded 2023

More Than a Stay.

Lunaire was created to be a place where people can pause, breathe, and experience something beautiful.

Our story

Founded in 2023.

Lunaire is a 4-star luxury boutique hotel resort in the scenic highlands of Tagaytay, inspired by the elegant and enchanting atmosphere of the Korean drama Hotel Del Luna. The concept was created from the belief that people deserve a sanctuary where they can step away from the noise and pressures of everyday life, find comfort, reflect, and create meaningful memories.

Inspired by the moon as a symbol of peace, hope, and quiet reflection, Lunaire was designed to transform an ordinary stay into an intimate and luxurious escape through elegant accommodations, personalized hospitality, dining, wellness, and leisure experiences.

More than simply offering a place to stay, Lunaire seeks to provide guests with a moment to slow down, let go of their worries, and reconnect with themselves and the people who matter to them. Whether for a peaceful getaway, romantic occasion, business trip, or celebration, every stay is designed to leave a lasting impression, fulfilling Lunaire's promise of being "Where Memories Shine Under the Moonlight."
