# 1. BRAND DIRECTION

## Brand Concept

Lunaire is a 4-star luxury boutique hotel resort inspired by the elegant, mysterious, and enchanting atmosphere associated with moonlit nights and the Korean drama *Hotel Del Luna*. The property is designed as a peaceful sanctuary where guests can temporarily step away from the demands of everyday life, rest, reconnect, celebrate, and create meaningful memories.
The website should communicate three things immediately:
**Luxury** — refined spaces, elegant details, premium service
**Emotion** — comfort, reflection, romance, meaningful moments
**Escape** — a feeling of entering another world away from daily pressures
The website should feel **premium, calm, cinematic, intimate, and mysterious**, rather than overly flashy.

---

# 2. VISUAL IDENTITY

## Recommended Website Colors

**Midnight Navy / Deep Charcoal**
Primary background and nighttime sections.
**Champagne Gold**
Buttons, highlights, borders, and elegant accents.
**Ivory / Warm White**
Main text areas and clean backgrounds.
**Dusty Lavender / Muted Mauve**
Small decorative accents that reinforce the moonlit atmosphere.
Use gold sparingly. Lunaire should feel sophisticated, not overly ornate.

## Typography

Use one elegant serif font for major headings and one clean sans-serif for body text.
**Headings:** Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond / Libre Baskerville
**Body:** Inter / Montserrat / Lato

## Photography Direction

Prioritize:
Nighttime exterior
Moonlit pool
Warm room lighting
Elegant beds
Large windows
Tagaytay scenery
Dining at night
Spa details
Couples enjoying private spaces
Candles / flowers / soft lighting
Architectural details
Close-up shots of luxury amenities
Avoid overly bright, generic stock-hotel photography.

# OUR STORY

### Founded in 2023

> Lunaire is a 4-star luxury boutique hotel resort in the scenic highlands of Tagaytay, inspired by the elegant and enchanting atmosphere of the Korean drama *Hotel Del Luna*. The concept was created from the belief that people deserve a sanctuary where they can step away from the noise and pressures of everyday life, find comfort, reflect, and create meaningful memories. Inspired by the moon as a symbol of peace, hope, and quiet reflection, Lunaire was designed to transform an ordinary stay into an intimate and luxurious escape through elegant accommodations, personalized hospitality, dining, wellness, and leisure experiences. More than simply offering a place to stay, Lunaire seeks to provide guests with a moment to slow down, let go of their worries, and reconnect with themselves and the people who matter. Whether for a peaceful getaway, romantic occasion, business trip, or celebration, every stay is designed to leave a lasting impression.

# 30. DESIGN RULES FOR THE WEBSITE

Do not put every piece of information on the first screen.
The first screen should create desire.
The second section should explain the offering.
The detailed information appears after the guest clicks.
For example:
**Homepage**

> Moonlight Suite
> *Where the night becomes your own.*
> ₱17,500/night
> **[VIEW SUITE]**

Then on the room page:

> 90 sqm
> 2 guests
> King Bed
> Panoramic View
> Private Balcony
> Freestanding Bathtub
> Full Amenities
> Included Services
> **[BOOK NOW]**

This creates a much more professional hotel experience.
